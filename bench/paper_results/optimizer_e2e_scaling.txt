Method
------
Release-built CPython 3.13 extension on an eight-core Apple M2 with 16 GB RAM.
The full factorial crosses exact 4/8/16/32/64 KiB serialized calls, 1/8/32
Python callers, and guarded-reference/admitted-joint-rewrite paths. Each cell
has five seeded-random-order replications of 1,024 timed calls, for 153,600
samples. One hundred untimed calls warm each cell. All callers share one hot
call site and one bounded audit queue. There are no provider or network calls.

Each call constructs and non-blockingly enqueues its audit row; one background
writer batches up to 128 rows. Replication-unique span IDs pair concurrent outer
durations with exact internal audit rows after an ordered flush. The flush and
SQLite commit are outside the timed group. The native writer is closed before
Python's separately linked SQLite reader is opened, avoiding process-scoped
POSIX lock interference between the two SQLite copies on macOS.

Guarded-reference p50 spans 0.077--0.104 ms at C=1 and 0.164--0.184 ms

Before/after diagnosis
----------------------
At matched C=32 cells, off-path persistence reduces p50 by 90.6--92.9% for
reference selection and 88.7--92.0% for admitted rewrites. Absolute throughput
is 3.06--3.97x and 2.60--3.58x the synchronous baseline, respectively. The
paired residual falls from 94--97% to 68--73% of mean time. This confirms that
synchronous SQLite persistence was the dominant median/throughput bottleneck.

It does not solve the tail. C=8 p99 reaches 2.315 ms and C=32 p99 reaches
16.776 ms, so the frozen 1.2 ms ship target still fails. Internal C=32 p99 is
0.418--0.784 ms for reference and 0.725--1.713 ms for admitted rewrites; normal
host scheduling, the FFI/state boundary, and 32 callers oversubscribing eight
logical CPUs remain inside the outer clock and require attribution. Replication
tail intervals remain materially wider than median intervals.

Audit and fallback accounting
-----------------------------
Across setup and timed calls, 170,700 rows were attempted, accepted, and
written. After every ordered flush there were zero pending rows, full-queue
drops, disconnected drops, or write failures. All 153,600 timed rows pair
exactly with raw samples.

Twenty-three of 76,800 admitted timed calls (0.030%) returned the immutable
reference with `planning_overhead_exceeded`. All occurred at C=32: 5/3/6/2/7
for 4/8/16/32/64 KiB. The harness preserves these events rather than aborting
or excluding them.

Evidence boundary
-----------------
Both artifacts have `paper_evidence=false`. They are zero-network,
single-machine Stage E0 diagnostics with synthetic fixed-structure ASCII
payloads. They measure request-byte scaling and a deliberately contended hot
site, not semantic complexity, provider latency, task quality, savings, or
multi-host behavior. The result validates the audit redesign and identifies a
remaining tail problem; it is not a confirmatory latency claim.
