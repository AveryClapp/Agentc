Method
------
Release-built CPython 3.13 extension on an eight-core Apple M2 with 16 GB RAM.
The full factorial crosses exact 4/8/16/32/64 KiB serialized calls, 1/8/32
Python callers, and guarded-reference/admitted-joint-rewrite paths. Each cell
has five seeded-random-order replications of 1,024 timed calls, for 153,600
samples. One hundred untimed calls warm each cell. All callers share one hot
call site and the production WAL-backed audit connection. There are no provider
or network calls.

Each timed call has a span ID unique within its timed replication. Its outer
`_native.optimize_plan` duration
is paired with the exact internal `plan_audit.overhead_us` row even when thread
completion and audit order differ. The native writer is closed after the timed
group and before the separately linked Python SQLite reader inspects its WAL;
that checkpoint is outside both clocks.

Guarded-reference p50 spans 0.108--0.135 ms at C=1 and 1.744--2.388 ms
at C=32. Its C=32 pooled p99 spans 14.620--41.436 ms and throughput speedup
is 1.35--1.69x. The admitted path's corresponding C=32 speedup is 1.38--1.98x,
only 4.3--6.2% efficiency relative to ideal 32-way scaling.

Interpretation
--------------
Sequential medians remain below 0.3 ms over the full 16x byte range. Under
contention, complete-call medians become millisecond-scale and p99 becomes
10--50 ms scale. Replication p50s are stable, while replication p99s and
throughput vary materially; the JSON therefore retains seeded 10,000-resample
95% intervals over replication-level values rather than presenting a single
tail as exact.

At C=32, the internal pre-audit planner p99 is 0.363--0.721 ms, but the paired
boundary/state/audit residual accounts for 94.2--96.9% of mean complete-call
time. The residual is not an audit-only timer. Still, the production code's
single mutex-protected synchronous audit connection is the known serialized
operation in that scope and is the first optimization target.

Two of 76,800 admitted-path calls (0.0026%) returned the immutable reference
with `planning_overhead_exceeded`: one 64 KiB/C=1 call and one 16 KiB/C=32 call.
The harness preserves these events rather than aborting or excluding them.

Evidence boundary
-----------------
This artifact has `paper_evidence=false`. It is a zero-network, single-machine
Stage E0 diagnostic with synthetic fixed-structure ASCII payloads. It measures
request-byte scaling and a deliberately contended hot site, not semantic
complexity, provider latency, task quality, savings, or multi-host behavior.
