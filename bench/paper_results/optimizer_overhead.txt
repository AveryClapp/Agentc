Method
------
Release-built CPython 3.13 extension on the recorded arm64 macOS host. Five
replications x 2,000 timed steady-state calls per scenario (10,000 samples per
row), with 200 untimed warmup calls per replication and rotated scenario order.
The workload is deterministic and synthetic, exploration and shadow execution
are disabled, SQLite uses WAL, and no provider or network call occurs.

The outer clock encloses the complete `_native.optimize_plan` call. Every sample
is paired one-to-one with the exact `plan_audit.overhead_us` row produced by the
call. That internal clock stops before audit persistence and excludes native
state lookup plus the outer FFI boundary.

Interpretation
--------------
On this host, the complete production planning call remains below 0.4 ms at p99
for all three measured steady-state paths. Boundary, state-lookup, persistence,
clock-quantization, and return work account for 58-61% of mean complete-call
time. Rare 13-40 ms maxima are retained rather than removed and materially raise
the means.

The residual is paired subtraction but is not an audit-only timer. The old
76 us / 120 us result aggregated 1,818 historical `plan_audit.overhead_us` rows
from different workloads and omitted the audit write plus outer FFI scope. It
must be described as an internal planner-clock diagnostic, not end-to-end
optimizer overhead.

Evidence boundary
-----------------
This artifact has `paper_evidence=false`. It is a zero-network, single-machine
Stage E0 systems diagnostic. It validates the timing mechanism and measured-host
budget but does not establish task quality, savings, provider latency, concurrent
contention, or a confirmatory campaign result.
