# Joint routing and semantic rewrites: novelty refresh

Research date: 2026-09-05. Bead: `bd-323l.6.13.8.6.21`. Repository evidence snapshot: `3b3e9b1`. Primary papers and official artifacts only; this is a bounded nearest-neighbor review, not an exhaustive novelty certificate. Source dates below distinguish first preprints, revisions, and archival publication. No inference or ledger access was performed for this review.

## Bottom line

The field has already occupied the broad idea of jointly optimizing models and prompts, including compound workflows. Runtime decisions, online model-quality learning, interaction-aware optimization, distribution-shift adaptation, and check-and-recover behavior also have substantial prior art. Combining those phrases is not enough for an MLSys contribution. The eight comparisons below support that assessment; they do **not** establish that one prior system implements every proposed Agentc mechanism.

A narrower research hypothesis remains worth testing: application-side execution of heterogeneous model-plus-rewrite plans, with online evidence tied to the exact call site and complete plan, persistent invalidation when that evidence becomes stale, and an executable contract that accounts for unsuccessful candidates and fallback. Its value must be demonstrated against strong alternatives, not inferred from an architectural checklist. The present artifacts do not yet do that.

## What the current experiment can establish

At the snapshot, the [typed-set pilot](../../bench/repro/openrouter-typed-set-pilot-2026-09-05/README.md) completed 54/54 calls with valid typed own-history execution. Those six tasks were adaptively exposed during protocol development. The target answered five of six correctly despite all six producing valid application state: **typed validity is not task correctness**. This is an execution/contract gate, not a held-out quality or joint-optimization result.

The [frozen factorial](../../bench/repro/openrouter-typed-factorial-2026-09-05/README.md), manifest `4e30338f35205059a2b3d5b32fe41ee2b771b4f35eeea18bc194b5730b2ac148`, has not been acquired at this snapshot. It specifies 32 fresh generated development tasks, four fixed model-by-rule arms, and three stages: 384 calls. Rules are materialized through real native rule methods, not admitted by the runtime optimizer. The native unknown-capacity route remains disabled; no fictitious context bound should be introduced to change that.

That factorial is the right **next diagnostic**: estimate paired task quality, invalid-state frequency, actual cost, and model-dependent rewrite effects with each arm's own history. Count failures rather than deleting them. Report the quality interaction `(Q_HR − Q_H0) − (Q_SR − Q_S0)` and cost ratios with uncertainty. An absolute dollar interaction caused solely by multiplying token reduction by different price rates is not evidence of learned synergy. If all arms are equally correct and Haiku plus rules is simply cheapest, the result supports a static configuration, not an adaptive controller. One synthetic development workload and one realization cannot establish generality, robust latency gains, or drift handling.

## Falsifiable contribution requirements

These are proposed tests, not firstness claims or completed work. Existing competitive-evaluation work is tracked in `bd-323l.3`; the missing CORAL review is the explicit follow-up above.

1. **An executable eligibility contract.** Keep the existing proven-bound path intact. Any opt-in unknown-capacity path must be separately versioned, disclose what is unknown, and produce measured overflow/fallback evidence. Report admission and abstention coverage. A native optimizer that never admits the relevant model choices cannot substantiate adaptive joint routing.

2. **Benefit beyond choosing a good static configuration.** Compare the best fixed model/rewrite plan, route-only, rewrite-only, both sequential selection orders, and a joint controller under the same paid budget and quality target. Separate selection data from evaluation. If the best static plan dominates after profiling costs, the adaptive claim fails. A subsequent confirmation should include at least two materially different workloads, including a public workload not constructed around the supported rules.

3. **A reason to learn complete plans.** Show cases where a model's capability changes after a rewrite and where compositions are beneficial or harmful beyond marginal predictions. Compare complete-plan evidence with independent model/rule profiles at equal observation budget. Calibrate stochastic disagreement with source-repeat measurements. No stable conditional differences means little justification for the proposed interaction-aware machinery.

4. **Semantic measurements that do not smuggle in an oracle.** Keep runtime application validity, agreement with a reference execution, and external task correctness separate. Preserve invalid own-history states and label blindness during dispatch. A valid typed response or agreement between two wrong executions is not correctness; a protocol repair introduced after observing failures is not confirmatory evidence.

5. **Persistent adaptation tested as an operational mechanism.** Introduce declared workload, prompt/schema, or provider-identity changes and restarts. Measure detection delay, accumulated damage, recovery cost, and stale-profile reuse against no-adaptation and nonpersistent controls. Demonstrate that evidence identity/invalidation changes outcomes; generic online retraining or a shuffled benchmark alone does not justify this narrower contribution.

6. **Charge the entire control loop and distinguish failure classes.** Include probes, shadow/reference calls, failed candidates, fallback, and controller overhead; report amortization and tail latency. Reserve candidate plus fallback before dispatch and test crash/replay behavior. Keep capacity/transport failures separate from semantic feedback; ambiguous failures and refusals must not silently become successful recovery. The research question is whether this explicit contract preserves useful savings under stated damage limits—not whether accounting or fallback was invented here.

## Decision

Continue with the reviewed fixed factorial before building a large adaptive feature. A useful conditional opportunity would justify the next native eligibility/control experiment; a null result should change the workload or research hypothesis, not the success criterion. The project remains relevant as a narrowly testable systems question, but the current evidence is **not MLSys main-track contribution proof**. This review narrows that claim rather than certifying novelty. CORAL's unavailable full methods and the absence of matched competitive runs remain material uncertainties.
